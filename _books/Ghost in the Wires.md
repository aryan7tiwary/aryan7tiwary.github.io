## Points to ponder:
1. He became so skilled because he worked on his interest without considering the consequences.

2. Always being ahead of enemies.

3.  Try to name some illegal activity that, with permission can be carried out legitimately and benefit others: *ethical hacking*.

4. World's Most Wanted Hacker to World's  Most Wanted Security Expert in the world.

5. Learning and Understanding cyberspace was his priority. Monetary benefits didn't affect him, even though he had access to 20 million credit cards.

6. The officials were too slow to catch him. It was someone he trusted; working for them.

7. The court proceedings were unfair to him. "Free Kevin" movement aroused at that time. By evaluating his crimes, he was being sentenced to prison for 460 years. He was denied of bail hearing, although US's most wanted criminals associated with cannibalism, murders and terrorism were given date for bail hearings. Eg: Jeffrey Dahmer. He was forced into [Solitary confinement]([Solitary confinement - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solitary_confinement)) which is declared inhumane now days.

8. No matter how bigger his problem was, the family always supported him.

9. At his hacking times, cyber attack was a new thing. Laws were not properly written.

10. A cyber-criminal earlier had hacked into banks and used stolen credit cards to withdraw money. He was jailed for 6 months with an order to keep away from computers for a while. This was a good point for KDM's case but he couldn't risk court opening his prior activities unknown to public. Kevin plead and was jailed for 64 months. He had already spent several months while the proceedings were going on. He spent some more days in prison and was ordered to not use computer for 8 years.

11. His wife Bonnie cheated on him which Kevin found using his cyber skills. He hacked into her voicemails and found out it was his hacking friend De Payne. KDM kept in touch with both of them. It was irony how Bonnie fighting with Kevin for his hacking addiction later bonded with someone with same addiction. 

12. One day at night, during high alert for the arrest of KDM, he walked out to his balcony for fresh air. It was unusual for a person to appear in the balcony so late at night. The FBI noticed and knocked at Kevin's door. Forced into his house, they questioned and started searching his house. Multiple IDs were found which made the officers more suspecious. Later on a letter to Kevin was found in his coat which confirmed their suspesion. He was arrested at that night.
