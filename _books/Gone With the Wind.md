## Key Characters

## Scarlett O'Hara

- Not traditionally beautiful, but charming and captivating
- More like her father than her younger sisters
- Selfish, willful, and determined
- Evolves throughout the story, realizing her true feelings late

## Rhett Butler

- Controversial figure in Atlanta society
- Complex character, often misunderstood by Scarlett
- Deeply loves Scarlett and Bonnie
- Cynical but insightful about human nature

## Ashley Wilkes

- Object of Scarlett's infatuation
- Honorable but indecisive
- Married to Melanie Hamilton

## Melanie Hamilton Wilkes

- Kind, gracious, and forgiving
- Loyal friend to Scarlett
- Dies near the end of the story

## Major Themes

## War and Its Aftermath

- Set during and after the American Civil War
- Depicts the fall of the Old South and struggles of Reconstruction

## Love and Relationships

- Complex romantic entanglements between characters
- Scarlett's evolving feelings for Ashley and Rhett

## Survival and Adaptation

- Characters struggling to survive in a changed world
- Scarlett's determination to save Tara
## Social Change

- Transformation of Southern society
- Changing roles of women and former slaves
